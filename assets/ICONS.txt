Place your icon files here:

  icon.png   — 512×512 PNG  (used by Linux & as fallback)
  icon.ico   — Windows ICO  (multi-size: 16,32,48,64,128,256)
  icon.icns  — macOS ICNS

Convert a PNG at:
  https://cloudconvert.com/png-to-ico
  https://cloudconvert.com/png-to-icns

The app will still build without icons — Electron uses a default.
